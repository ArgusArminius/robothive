Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Effective date: September 11, 2026 · Last updated: September 11, 2026

behindrobotics.com ("Behind Robotics," "we," "us," or "our") publishes news, analysis, and a curated database covering the robotics, drone, and embodied-AI industry. This policy explains what information we collect — from visitors to this site, and, separately, from public sources for our database and editorial content — why we collect it, the legal grounds we rely on, and the choices and rights you have, including under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

1. Who we are (data controller)

For the purposes of the GDPR, the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy is:

Christian Sieling, operating behindrobotics.com

[Registered business name and postal address to be added here]

Contact: contact form or christian@behindrobotics.com

Given the scale and nature of our processing, we don't currently have a statutory obligation to appoint a Data Protection Officer. The contact above handles all privacy questions and data-rights requests directly.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Newsletter signup — when you subscribe, we collect your email address and, optionally, your name. Signups are processed through our content and membership platform, Ghost (hosted at robothive.ghost.io), which sends you a confirmation/login link and delivers the newsletter.
  • Contact form — when you use our contact form, we collect your first and last name, email address, company (optional), the topic you select, and your message, so we can respond to you.

Information collected automatically:

  • Standard server/access logs — like most websites, our hosting and content-delivery infrastructure automatically logs technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page, and timestamps. This is used for security, abuse prevention, and understanding aggregate site usage — not to identify individual visitors.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see Section 5 below.

We do not collect financial account numbers, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data through this site, and we do not knowingly collect more than the fields listed above. We do not intentionally collect or process special categories of personal data (such as data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, health, or sexual orientation) through this site.

3. Personal data in our company database and editorial content

Separately from the visitor data above, Behind Robotics compiles and publishes an industry database and editorial content about companies in the robotics, drone, and embodied-AI sector. In doing so, we may process a limited amount of personal data about individuals connected to those companies — most commonly the names and professional titles of founders, executives, or company spokespeople.

This data is not collected from those individuals directly; it is drawn from public sources such as company websites, press releases, funding and investment announcements, regulatory filings, and news coverage. Consistent with GDPR Article 14, we disclose here: the categories of data involved (name and professional role/title, and occasionally a public professional biography); that our source is publicly available business and news information, not the individual; and that we do not typically hold contact details, financial information, or any other personal data about these individuals beyond what is already public and relevant to their professional or business role.

Our legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in independent business and technology journalism and in maintaining an accurate, public-interest industry reference database, weighed against the individual's rights and interests. If you are named in our database or editorial content and believe our information about you is inaccurate, out of date, or you wish to object to this processing, contact us using the details in Section 11 and we will review your request.

4. Legal basis for processing

  • Newsletter signup — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Contact form — your consent in submitting the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries sent to us (Art. 6(1)(a) and 6(1)(f)).
  • Server/access logs — our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure, functioning, and free of abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Non-essential cookies — your consent via our cookie banner (Art. 6(1)(a)); strictly necessary/essential cookies (such as remembering your cookie choice itself) do not require consent.
  • Company database and editorial content — our legitimate interest in public-interest journalism and an accurate industry database (Art. 6(1)(f)); see Section 3.

We do not use your personal data for any automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar local-storage technologies: essential ones needed for the site to function (such as remembering your cookie preference itself), and optional ones associated with embedded third-party content (such as YouTube videos) once you've accepted them. When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies; you can change your choice at any time by clearing the site's local storage in your browser or by contacting us.

6. Third-party services and recipients

Operating this site involves a small number of third-party services, each of which processes a limited, specific set of data:

  • Ghost (robothive.ghost.io) — our content management and newsletter/membership platform. Processes newsletter subscriber name/email and may set its own cookies for members.
  • Finnhub — powers the live market-quote ticker and Investment Monitor pricing data shown on this site. We query Finnhub's public market-data API directly from your browser to fetch stock quotes; no personal information about you is sent to Finnhub as part of this.
  • YouTube (Google) — embedded video content may set its own cookies and collect viewing data when you play a video, governed by Google's own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. International data transfers

Some of the third-party services listed in Section 6 — including Ghost, Finnhub, and Google/YouTube — may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or your own country, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on legally recognized transfer mechanisms, such as the European Commission's adequacy decisions (for example, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the recipient is certified under it) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any additional safeguards required by law. International transfer mechanisms are subject to ongoing legal and regulatory developments; if you'd like more detail on a specific transfer, contact us using the details in Section 11.

8. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the site and its data/analytics features
  • To send the newsletter you signed up for, and to respond to messages sent through our contact form
  • To compile, curate, and publish our industry database, editorial content, and market/investment analytics
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or unauthorized automated access

9. How long we keep information

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your inquiry. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Server/access logs are retained for a limited period for security purposes and then routinely deleted or aggregated. Personal data appearing in our company database and editorial content (Section 3) is retained for as long as it remains accurate and relevant to the company record it describes, and is corrected or removed on request as described in Section 3.

10. Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or another jurisdiction with similar protections, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten"), in certain circumstances
  • Restrict our processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interest (including the database/editorial processing in Section 3), or to any direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal (such as unsubscribing from our newsletter via the link in any email)
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — in your EU/UK country of residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We'll respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

11. Contact us

For any question about this Privacy Policy, to exercise your data rights, or to flag a concern about personal data in our database or editorial content, please reach out via our contact form, selecting the "Other" topic, or write to us directly.

12. Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general business and industry audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the site evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do; material changes will be noted on this page.

This policy reflects a good-faith effort to meet GDPR's Article 13/14 disclosure requirements based on the site's actual data practices, and is not a substitute for legal advice. The registered business name and postal address in Section 1 still need to be added, and if this site processes data from residents of other regulated jurisdictions (for example the UK GDPR, California's CCPA/CPRA, or others), we recommend having this policy reviewed by a qualified privacy lawyer to confirm full compliance.
