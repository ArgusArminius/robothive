AI Act — high-risk robotics

EU · In force

Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230

EU · In force

The core robot law in the EU. Requires CE marking, a Declaration of Conformity and a technical file before sale or putting into service. Still certifiable under Directive 2006/42/EC during the transition period. Harmonised safety standards EN ISO 10218-1/-2 and ISO/TS 15066 evidence conformity and safe human-robot collaboration.

Official source

Market Surveillance Regulation (EU) 2019/1020

EU · In force

Article 4 requires an EU-established economic operator accountable for product compliance — meaning a manufacturer, importer or authorised representative inside the EU must hold responsibility for robots placed on the market.
